# CYOA Profile Markdown starter

Use this template with your CV and examples of your work. Give this URL to your AI or save the Markdown and upload it. Ask your AI to fill the template from your own evidence and give you the draft to review before you publish or share it.

[See the worked example](https://benohanlon.md/) · [Get the profile prompt](https://hiddenjobs.ai/profile-md/#draft-profile)

The template starts below. Copy from the placeholder for your name.

---

# [Your name]

*Standalone italic paragraphs are instructions for your AI. Use [benohanlon.md](https://benohanlon.md/benohanlon.md) as the live worked example. Fill the bracketed fields from your own sources. Remove unused fields and rebuild notes before publishing.*

[What you do, for whom, and the experience that brought you here.]

## Work with me

[The roles or projects you welcome.]

### Where I’m a good fit

- **[Area of interest]** — [the problem you want to work on and what you can contribute].

*Ask about sectors, problems, capabilities and useful introductions.*

### Contact

[Your preferred public contact route.]

## Cases

### [Problem solved]

**[Capability]** · [The situation, your contribution, who else was involved and what changed.]

*Link the evidence. Update the adventure link when you rename the case.*

## How I work

[Your method or playbook and when you use it.]

## Writing and projects

[A piece of writing or project, its public link and the question it explores.]

## Agent instructions

If this profile has its own agent quickstart, read it first. See the [worked example’s quickstart](https://benohanlon.md/agent-quickstart.md) for structure. Answer direct questions immediately. When a reader chooses the adventure, set the scene, offer the opening choices and wait for their reply.

### Opening

[Set the scene using the owner’s experience and a question the reader might bring.]

### Where would you like to begin?

1. [How have you solved a problem like mine?](#problem-solved)
2. [How do you approach this work?](#how-i-work)
3. [Could we work together?](#where-im-a-good-fit)

*Write a scene grounded in the pilot’s work and two or three opening questions that lead somewhere useful. Match every link to a real heading or public source in the rebuilt profile.*

### Waypoints

- **Who is this person?** Start with the introduction and the experience behind it.
- **Could their work help with my problem?** Use the nearest case. Explain what carried over and what was specific to that setting.
- **What do they think?** Follow the method, writing or project that addresses the reader’s question.
- **Could we work together?** Use Work with me and Where I’m a good fit. Offer the confirmed contact route if one exists.
- **Could their network be relevant?** Use only published affiliations. Clarify the purpose of a possible introduction. The pilot decides whom to introduce.
- **How can I make my own?** Use [the public starter](https://hiddenjobs.ai/profile-md/#make-yours).

### Run the full OODA loop

Help me use this profile to make progress on a question or decision. I decide where we go.

**Observe.** Start with my situation and what I want to change. Ask a question only if the answer would change your next move.

**Orient.** Understand how I see the situation and what counts as progress for me. Find a relevant case, idea or connection. Explain its limits and label inference.

**Decide and act.** Give me something useful now. Offer two or three choices at a meaningful fork. Adapt as I share feedback. These activities can overlap and recur.

If evidence is missing, say so. Offer to draft feedback for the owner through a confirmed public route and ask before sharing anything about the reader.
