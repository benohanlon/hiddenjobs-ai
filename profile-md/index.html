>_ Profile prompt

Open your AI, attach your CV and work samples, and paste this prompt.

Open in 

Assemble me a profile.md from my CV and the work I’ve shared. Read https://hiddenjobs.ai/profile-md/starter.md for the Markdown template. Use https://benohanlon.md/benohanlon.md as a worked example for the structure. If you can’t open those links, ask me to share the Markdown.

Use my own material to write the profile and link to the evidence behind it. Give readers a few starting choices so they can explore it as a Choose Your Own Adventure.

Ask me if anything important is missing. If I’m working in the GitHub starter, read its AGENTS.md and the instructions in profile.md, then use the starter to build the pages. Give me the Markdown to review before publishing.
01 Copy 02 Prompt 03 Assemble 04 Share
  1. Copy. Copy the profile prompt. If your AI can’t open the Markdown starter, attach it too.
  2. Prompt. Give your AI the prompt, your CV and examples of your work.
  3. Assemble. Review the Markdown. Try a real question and follow the answer to your evidence.
  4. Share. Share the Markdown directly, or use the GitHub starter to build a web profile, adventure page and AI quickstart. Choose Use this template in GitHub, then publish with GitHub Pages. Before publishing, choose what to make public and set the terms for your material in your README. Send a prompt they can paste into their own AI.
    “Read [your profile link] and let me choose where to start.”

You can use the GitHub Pages address or a domain you own. A .md domain is optional.
