Let people start with their own question

Share your profile.md alongside your CV, with an application or after an introduction. People can open it in their own AI, ask follow-up questions and get straight to the examples that matter to them, on their own time.

They could ask

  • What problems could you solve for me?
  • Who are you and how could I work with you?
  • How could you connect me to your network?
  • Where would we have mutual interests or shared communities?

Tried it? Tell us what you asked and where you got stuck.

Assemble your profile

The starter builds your web profile, adventure page and AI quickstart from one Markdown file.

Use the GitHub starter
  1. Copy. Choose Use this template on GitHub and open your copy with an AI that can edit its files.
  2. Prompt. Give your AI your CV and work samples, then copy the profile prompt into your AI.
  3. Assemble. Review the Markdown and preview the generated pages. Try a real question and follow the answer to your evidence.
  4. Share. Share the Markdown directly or publish the site with GitHub Pages and send the link. Before publishing, choose what to make public and set the terms for your own material in your README.

You can use the GitHub Pages address or a domain you own. A .md domain is optional.

Stuck with the kit? Ask for help on GitHub.

Build on it as you go

Add new work, better examples and what you’re aiming for next. Use the same file to give your own AI context about you and what you’re trying to do.

>_ Profile prompt

Use this in chat to draft your Markdown, or in your copy of the GitHub starter to build the full profile.

Assemble me a profile.md from my CV and the work I’ve shared. Use https://benohanlon.md/benohanlon.md as a worked example for the structure.

Use my own material to write the profile and link to the evidence behind it. Give readers a few starting choices so they can explore it as a Choose Your Own Adventure.

Ask me if anything important is missing. If I’m working in the GitHub starter, read its AGENTS.md and the instructions in profile.md, then use the starter to build the pages. Give me the Markdown to review before publishing.
